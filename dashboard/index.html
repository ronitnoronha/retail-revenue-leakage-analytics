Churn: 7 models from 6 families vs a simple rule

ROC-AUC: how often a random churner is ranked above a random non-churner (0.5 = coin flip). Dot: out-of-time test (10 Sep – 9 Dec 2011). Line: cross-validation mean ± 1 sd, grouped by customer. All models land close together, so the simplest one, logistic regression, was chosen.

Cancellation models

PR-AUC suits a rarer outcome. Models find more cancelling customers than the spend rule, but the spend rule catches slightly more cancelled £, because the biggest cancellations come from the biggest buyers.

Which 5% of accounts to flag for order confirmation

Flagging the biggest accounts adds friction to almost half of revenue. Flagging past cancellers covers a similar share of cancelled £ while touching far less revenue, so it is the recommended policy.
